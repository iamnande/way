# way

> "which way is my way?"

this `way` is my way.

it's tailor made to capture the implied aspects of a well-rounded existence on
the third rock from the sun.

it is the answer to: _"who are you?"_, set from a senior age set of goals.

in effect - create your character's old self, then rewind it. what decisions are
required along the way to get there?

## ontology

there are a distilled set of `pillars` to support a well-earned old age defined
in `way`. held true by a set of `principles` to guide decision making along the
way.

1. `mind` / what has your attention? where have you invested in your mental
   health? good answers come easy with well-formed questions. ask them along the
   way - in a `journal`.

we `write` in a `journal`, adding new entries somewhere close to `daily`.

we `read` these `journal entries` from time to time.

on a cadence, we are `prompted` for a `check-in` to see how we perceive our
goals, progress, and any impacts to our long-term goals.

some days we'll forget something and `search` for something in our `journal`.

2. `body` / how is your health? (note: this might very well require a real-ass
   medical doctor visit more than once a decade along the way) are you being
   intentional about your sleep?

we create `exercise routines`, mostly to follow the sun - life is too short to
choose to run in the rain consistently.

`exercise routine` are filled with `exercises`, with each `exercise` containing
attributes like `sets`, `reps`, `intensity`, `friction` - all leading to a
combined `exercise duration` which are then aggregated to an
`exercise routine duration`, `intensity`, and `friction`.

we also `sleep`, like - every night (well...). which `variation` did you sleep
in? do you feel rested? what is the `duration` you slept?

we `review` our `sleep` history on a cadence, to ensure time is served in a
healthy and efficient manner. this is also useful for....

`medical appointment` shouldn't be "just" a thing scheduled when needed. the
body is in a constaint state of deteriation in many "fun" and "interesting"
ways.

this is the mechanics of our biology - grease it regularly, with intentionality.

these `medical appointments` are typically associated with a specific
`provider`, which have an `address`, `phone-number`, and `current-insurance`.

(because single payer is just "soooo" much to ask for, amirite? aka indicator of
who to phone when insurance changes until the US can get their collective shit
together)

each `medical appointment` has a `date`, scheduled `time`, `purpose` for
scheduling and space for `documents` needed.

not only that, we have responsibilities to schedule `medical visits` for those
we share a form `relationship` with.

3. `relationships` / where can we invest in the kids? my parter? my communities
   along the way?

we have `children`, and each `child` is unique in their own right. since their
`birthdate`, they've grown `dreams`/`aspirations`, `hobbies`, `"preferred X"`,
(food, pens, gatorade flavor, etc.) and `areas requiring extra attention`.

we have a `partner` in life - capture everything. invest. STONKS.

the `family` sometimes `travels` to far-away lands. these `travels` have a
`plan` - with everything from a movement order to local food options captured.

4. `craft` / what is "your space"? how does this compare with the market? what
   is our trajectory along the way and why?

5. `stability` / how are your finances? what's our housing plan (moving plan)?
   how can we improve resilience along the way?

6. `purpose` / why do we invest in any & all of the above items, perpetually,
   along the way?
